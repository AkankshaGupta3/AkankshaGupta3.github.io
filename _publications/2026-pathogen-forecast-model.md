Open access. The Pathogen Forecast Model (PFM) couples regional ocean circulation,
wave, and tracer transport models to produce five-day beach water quality
forecasts for the San Diego/Tijuana region. My contribution centered on
validating model dye predictions against ddPCR-measured enterococcus
observations at Imperial Beach shoreline stations.
