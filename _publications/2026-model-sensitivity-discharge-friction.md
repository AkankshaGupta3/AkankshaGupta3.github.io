**Status: submitted, September 2026.**

Coupled model predictions of nearshore salinity and wastewater tracer transport
depend strongly on two uncertain inputs: the freshwater discharge from the
Tijuana River Estuary and the bottom friction parameterization in the surfzone.
This study quantifies that sensitivity and identifies which choices matter most
for beach-scale water quality forecasting.
