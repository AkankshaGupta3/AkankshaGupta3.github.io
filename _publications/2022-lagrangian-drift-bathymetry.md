An alongshore current flowing over a rippled or corrugated seabed generates a
steady Lagrangian drift directed across the shelf &mdash; even with no surface
waves present. The mechanism has direct implications for cross-shelf transport
of pollutants, sediment, and microplastics in coastal oceans.
